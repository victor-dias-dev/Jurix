# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want a community where people can report bugs, disagree about the contract workflow, and contribute without harassment. We pledge to make participation free of harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to this community:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use Jurix
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/victor-dias-dev/Jurix/security/advisories/new) so the report is not public. Maintainers will review the report and may remove comments, commits, or code, or ban a contributor from the community spaces of this project.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
